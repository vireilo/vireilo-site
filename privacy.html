Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Vireilo Management LLC is a separate legal entity serving as the management arm of Vireilo Ventures LLC. “Vireilo Ventures Management” is the public-facing designation of Vireilo Management LLC. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you visit our website or communicate with us. For the SMS/Text Messaging provisions below, “we,” “us,” and “our” specifically mean Vireilo Management LLC, the registered sender and operator of the messaging program.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Information you provide — name, email address, company, mobile phone number, messaging preferences, consent records, and message content when you contact us or request text communication.
  • Automatically collected data — standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and approximate location. We do not use this for individual profiling.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — essential cookies for site function. We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics cookies that track you across sites.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and maintain correspondence.
  • Send conversational and operational text messages that you have requested, including responses to inquiries, scheduling, property and service communications, and account or transaction notifications.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

We do not sell personal information.

3. Sharing of Information

We may share information with service providers who assist in operating the website or email systems, under confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Vireilo Ventures or others.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that help us deliver and support the messaging program. Those providers may use the information only to perform services for Vireilo Management LLC.

4. Data Retention

We retain correspondence and related records only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by law.

We retain mobile numbers, messaging preferences, and records of consent for as long as needed to operate the messaging program, document compliance, resolve disputes, and satisfy applicable legal requirements.

5. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by contacting us at hello@vireilo.com. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

7. SMS/Text Messaging

You may enroll by visiting our public SMS Opt-In page, entering your mobile number, and selecting the unchecked consent box. The form records your submission, consent, and the version of the disclosure shown when you enrolled.

Vireilo Management LLC sends conversational and operational messages and may identify itself publicly as Vireilo Ventures Management. Messages may include responses to inquiries, scheduling information, property and service communications, and account or transaction notifications. Vireilo Ventures LLC is not the registered sender for this messaging program. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. We may send one final confirmation message after receiving your request. Reply HELP for assistance or contact hello@vireilo.com. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

For additional program terms, see our Terms of Service. You may enroll through our SMS Opt-In page.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
